Roadmap to Success

15-minute check-in appointment

As your Account Manager my goal is to do everything I can to make sure your expectations are being fully met for the investment you have made.

At the 15-minute check-in meeting, we will:

  • Make sure all products you have purchased have been successfully deployed and have fully met your expectations
  • Review best practices on how to maximize the benefits of the products you purchased
  • Do a status check on how you are progressing against your business and growth objectives
  • Discuss whether there is anything else we can do to augment your existing marketing, to help you achieve your objectives
